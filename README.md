## Problem Title
SIH 25010: Smart Crop Advisory System for Small and Marginal Farmers
## Problem Description
A majority of small and marginal farmers in India rely on traditional knowledge, local shopkeepers, or guesswork for crop selection, pest control, and fertilizer use. They lack access to personalized, real-time advisory services that account for soil type, weather conditions, and crop history. This often leads to poor yield, excessive input costs, and environmental degradation due to overuse of chemicals. Language barriers, low digital literacy, and absence of localized tools further limit their access to modern agri-tech resources.

Impact / Why this problem needs to be solved

Helping small farmers make informed decisions can significantly increase productivity, reduce costs, and improve livelihoods. It also contributes to sustainable farming practices, food security, and environmental conservation. A smart advisory solution can empower farmers with scientific insights in their native language and reduce dependency on unreliable third-party advice.

Expected Outcomes

• A multilingual, AI-based mobile app or chatbot that provides real-time, location-specific crop advisory.
• Soil health recommendations and fertilizer guidance.
• Weather-based alerts and predictive insights.
• Pest/disease detection via image uploads.
• Market price tracking.
• Voice support for low-literate users.
• Feedback and usage data collection for continuous improvement.

Relevant Stakeholders / Beneficiaries

• Small and marginal farmers
• Agricultural extension officers
• Government agriculture departments
• NGOs and cooperatives
• Agri-tech startups

Supporting Data

• 86% of Indian farmers are small or marginal (NABARD Report, 2022).
• Studies show ICT-based advisories can increase crop yield by 20–30%.

## Proposed Solution
<h3>agro savo namho</h3>
<ul><li>Detailed explanation of the proposed solution
We propose a multilingual, AI-powered Smart Crop Advisory Platform that delivers real-time, location-specific recommendations to farmers through a user-friendly mobile application and voice-enabled chatbot.

Key Features:

Personalized Crop Advisory: Recommendations based on soil type, weather conditions, and crop history.

Soil Health & Fertilizer Guidance: Optimal input recommendations to reduce overuse and costs.

Weather Alerts & Predictive Insights: Timely notifications for irrigation, sowing, and harvesting.

Pest & Disease Detection: AI-enabled image recognition to identify and suggest preventive measures.

Market Price Tracking: Real-time access to local mandi prices for better selling decisions.

Multilingual & Voice Support: Ensuring accessibility for low-literate and non-English-speaking farmers.

Feedback & Data Analytics: Continuous improvement of recommendations based on farmer input.</li>
<li>How it addresses the problem
Lack of Scientific Advisory → Personalized AI Recommendations

Farmers currently rely on guesswork or third-party advice.

Our system provides data-driven, personalized crop and fertilizer recommendations using AI models that analyze soil type, weather forecasts, and crop history.

Overuse of Fertilizers and Pesticides → Smart Input Guidance

Excessive use of chemicals reduces soil fertility and increases costs.

The platform offers optimal fertilizer and pesticide suggestions, reducing wastage and promoting sustainable practices.

Unpredictable Weather → Real-Time Alerts & Forecasting

Weather variability leads to crop losses.

The system integrates with real-time weather APIs to give farmers timely alerts (rainfall, drought, frost, or heatwaves) and predictive insights for sowing, irrigation, and harvesting.

Crop Diseases and Pest Attacks → AI Image Recognition

Farmers struggle to identify early-stage pests or diseases.

By uploading field images, farmers can use AI-based pest/disease detection, with localized treatment recommendations.

Price Fluctuations in Markets → Market Price Tracking

Farmers often sell at lower rates due to lack of price transparency.

The app provides daily mandi price updates so farmers can make informed selling decisions.

Low Digital Literacy & Language Barriers → Multilingual Voice Support

Many small farmers are not comfortable with text-heavy or English-only platforms.

The advisory is provided in regional languages with voice-enabled support, ensuring inclusivity and ease of use.

Fragmented Feedback → Continuous Improvement

Current advisory channels do not adapt to farmer needs.

Our system captures farmer feedback and usage data to continuously refine the advisory, making it more localized and effective over time.</li>
<li>Innovation and uniqueness of the solution
AI + IoT Integration for Small Farmers

While many agri-tech solutions exist, they are often designed for large-scale farming.

Our solution uniquely combines AI/ML models with affordable IoT soil sensors to provide real-time, field-specific insights tailored to small and marginal farmers.

Multilingual, Voice-First Design

Unlike typical advisory apps, our platform is voice-enabled and available in regional languages, ensuring inclusivity for farmers with low literacy and limited digital skills.

AI-Powered Pest & Disease Detection

Farmers can simply upload an image of their crop, and the system will use computer vision to detect pests/diseases and suggest remedies — eliminating dependency on local shopkeepers or guesswork.

Offline-First Functionality

Many rural areas face poor connectivity.

The app is designed with offline capability, storing advisory data locally and syncing automatically when internet access is available.

Market Intelligence for Farmers

Beyond agronomy, the system empowers farmers with real-time mandi price tracking, helping them sell crops at the best possible value — a feature missing in most advisory platforms.

Continuous Learning & Personalization

The platform improves over time by collecting feedback and usage data.

AI models are retrained with local insights, ensuring recommendations remain dynamic, adaptive, and region-specific.

Holistic One-Stop Platform

Unlike fragmented services (separate apps for soil, weather, pest, or mandi prices), this solution offers an all-in-one advisory system, reducing complexity for farmers.</li></ul>

## Feasibility and Viability

<ul><li>Analysis of the feasibility of the idea
Technical Feasibility

Proven Technologies: AI/ML, NLP, IoT sensors, and cloud computing are already mature and widely used in other sectors. Adapting them for agriculture is technically feasible.

Scalability: Cloud-based infrastructure ensures that the platform can scale from pilot projects to nationwide deployment.

Device Compatibility: An Android-first mobile app with offline-first design ensures accessibility, as most rural farmers use entry-level smartphones.

2. Economic Feasibility

Low-Cost Deployment: Basic IoT soil sensors and open APIs (weather, mandi prices) keep costs manageable.

Support Mechanisms: Government subsidies, NGO support, and partnerships with agri-tech startups can reduce deployment costs.

Revenue Models: Freemium model (basic advisory free, premium insights for cooperatives/agribusinesses) or government-funded schemes ensure sustainability.

3. Operational Feasibility

Ease of Use: Multilingual, voice-enabled, and intuitive interfaces ensure adoption even among digitally less literate farmers.

Offline Functionality: Critical advisories available without internet reduce dependency on rural network availability.

Stakeholder Collaboration: Partnerships with agriculture departments, extension officers, and cooperatives make on-ground implementation practical.

4. Socio-Cultural Feasibility

Language & Literacy Barriers Addressed: Regional language support and voice-first advisory make the system inclusive.

Trust Building: Collaborations with local extension officers and NGOs increase farmer trust and adoption.

Behavioral Adoption: Early pilot studies show farmers are receptive when advisories are personalized, actionable, and delivered in familiar ways.

5. Risk & Mitigation

Connectivity Issues → Offline-first design.

Farmer Resistance to Technology → Training workshops and field demonstrations.

Data Privacy Concerns → Secure storage and compliance with government data protection norms.

Hardware Cost → Affordable IoT kits subsidized through government/NGO partnerships.
